### Keyboard bug found and fixed
The page ignored keys whenever any `<input>` had focus. One click on the speed
slider or a checkbox, and WASD / arrows stopped driving (the arrows moved the
slider instead), and **Space / Esc STOP did nothing**. Capital letters didn't
work either. Now keys work everywhere except while typing in a text box; Esc
stops even then; Space stops instead of clicking a focused button; held keys
light up the pad.

### `web_ui` backend
- Subscribes `/rosout` (every node's log, last 2000 kept), `/cmd_vel_safe` (what
  the wheels are told), the IMU gyro, and counts lidar / IMU / odom message
  rates.
- Keeps the robot's trail (point every 5 cm; position jumps from slam_toolbox
  corrections don't count as distance), ground speed, area mapped, time since
  Start mapping, and Pi health from `/proc` and `/sys` (CPU temperature, CPU %,
  load, memory).
- Each radar payload carries the pose the scan was taken from, so the page can
  draw the lidar on the map without smear.
- New endpoints `/api/logs`, `/api/trail`, `/api/launchlog`; `/api/status`
  gains `telemetry`, `trail`, `robot_radius_m` (the launch output moved out of
  it to keep the 1 s poll small).
- Takeover: a drive command while a mission runs sends `stop` to the mission,
  then drives.
- Pure helpers in `logic.py` (`RateMeter`, `Trail`, `mapped_area_m2`, the
  `/proc` parsers, `log_level_name`) and `MappingProcess.uptime_s`, all tested:
  `lucia_web` now has 60 tests.
