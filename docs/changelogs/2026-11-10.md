## Decisions

- **One Pico 2 carries the IMU and the ToF sensors** (2026-11-10). It keeps the name `/dev/imu`
  (the Pi's udev rule matches its serial number), so the Pi's rules don't change for it.
- **`tof_node` reads both Pico programs**: Noel's `src/pico2/main.py` (binary packets: IMU at
  100 Hz with acceleration, plus ToF) and Ling's `src/imu/stream_main.py` (`I,...` text lines, IMU
  only, what's on the robot's Pico now). The robot keeps its heading whichever is loaded, so loading
  Noel's code later needs no switch anywhere.
- `/imu/data` from `tof_node` matches what `imu_bridge` sent (frame `imu_link`, same variances,
  stamped on arrival), so the EKF settings don't change. `imu_bridge` stays in the repo for
  checking the IMU by hand, but nothing starts it.
- **Wake both lines.** The new cable has BRC on RTS (Noel tested it); the old one used DTR, if
  wired at all. Pulsing both together works with either cable, so the change no longer has to
  wait for the new cable to be installed, as Noel's commit note said.
- "ToF safety stop" is **off by default**: with it on and the sensors not sending, forward driving
  is blocked (missing data counts as danger, by design).

### The IMU is upside down
- That IMU packet read gravity as −9.87 m/s² on the board's z axis (the right way up reads about
  +9.8) and the orientation as about 180° about y: the board is chip side down. `ekf.yaml` takes
  heading and turn rate only from the IMU (the wheels give forward speed), so the turn rate would
  have come out backwards. The page's turn-rate reading and the stuck check read `/imu/data` too.
- It can't be turned over, so `tof_node` turns the readings over before publishing (Christian's
  call): orientation × 180° about x, y and z of the turn rates and accelerations negated
  (`imu.turned_over`). Either way of turning it over gives the right heading changes and turn rate.
- Which way up: from gravity in the first reading with Noel's `main.py`, logged ("IMU board is
  upside down: turning its readings over"); nothing is published before that, so the EKF's starting
  heading never comes from an unturned reading. `stream_main.py` sends no acceleration, so then
  `imu_upside_down` decides (true in `config/tof.yaml`); a mismatch with gravity is logged. After
  about a second without a clear answer (board on its side), the setting decides.
- Tests: 4 new, including the captured reading and a simulated left turn with the board turned
  over either way (raw: turn rate backwards; turned over: heading and rate turn left). Stub-ROS run:
  the robot's packet publishes +9.87 and a left turn as +0.5 rad/s. Not yet on the robot.
- `lucia_tof/udev/99-lucia-tof.rules` and the udev block in `ros2-pi.md` list the new serials.
