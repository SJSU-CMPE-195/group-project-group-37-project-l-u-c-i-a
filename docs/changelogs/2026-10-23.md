### Tight spaces: "it stutters, but eventually figures it out"
Looked at everything acting on the robot there: Nav2's path follower (Regulated Pure Pursuit) and
its collision check, Nav2's recovery steps (clear the map, spin 90°, wait 5 s, back up 30 cm),
`safety_gate`'s slowdown, and `roomba_bridge`'s speed ramp.
- **Found a fault in the ramp** (added 2026-10-17): it eased forward speed and turn rate
  separately. Turning stops in a tenth of a second, driving takes up to half a second, so on a stop
  from a curve the robot ran straight past it: a 0.5 m curve opened to 0.8 m, then about 4 cm
  straight. In a 0.7 m doorway (about 12 cm to spare each side) that's toward the frame. Now both
  ease together and the robot stays on the curve (`motion.ramp_twist`, 6 new tests).
- **Nav2 look-ahead scales with speed** (`use_velocity_scaled_lookahead_dist: true`, 1.5 s, between
  0.25 and 0.7 m; was a fixed 0.4 m). Slowed in a tight space it aims 0.25 m ahead: less corner
  cutting, and the collision check, which looks no further than that point, stops flagging a frame
  it would pass. In the open at 30 cm/s: 0.45 m.
- Not changed yet: Nav2's recovery steps. If it still stutters, the logs from one tight spot
  (Warnings and up) will show whether they run; if they do, a faster recovery without the 5 s wait
  is next.
